## Dates and citations

- The visible year is the conference or journal volume year. Every `date` is January 1 of that year for sorting; it is **not an asserted exact publication date**. Within-year ordering is therefore not a chronology.
- SNOPS uses **IJCV 2025**, volume 133, pages 527–548. The publisher also reports online publication on 7 August 2024. This is one paper, listed once under the final journal year.
- The triplane tokenization paper is **RA-L 2025**, as verified on NVIDIA ASPIRE. The NVIDIA page's June 2025 date is retained here only as source context; no precise journal publication day is claimed.
- BibTeX blocks are minimal citations assembled from verified title, full author order, venue and year. Unverified page ranges, DOI values and issue numbers are not invented. They can be replaced with publisher BibTeX when desired.
- Preprints of the listed conference/journal papers are linked for access but not duplicated as separate publications.
- Original scientific figures are copied from the authors' project repositories, their research labs, arXiv HTML, or the publisher. No synthetic research graphics were generated. Their original rights remain with the respective authors/publishers; they are not covered by any site-code license.
- Older project URLs on `saltoricristiano.github.io` were omitted because replacing the root site could invalidate those paths. Verified code repositories are linked where available.
